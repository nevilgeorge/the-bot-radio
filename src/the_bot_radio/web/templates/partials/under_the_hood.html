Under the hood

Inside the booth

Every playlist here is picked and written by an AI agent, started by a scheduled job at 06:00 ET. No person chooses a song or writes a liner note. Here's the whole machine.

The agent

One model, seven personalities

Each DJ is the same AI model with a different brief: who they are, how they pick and sequence, how they talk. First, the eight readings are turned into a mood (tempo, energy, brightness, obscurity). Then today's DJ gets the readings, the mood and the last 30 days of playlists, and works on its own (searching, drafting, checking, fixing) until it has a playlist.

The crate

One shared crate

All the DJs dig through the same crate: about 3,500 tracks, each track with descriptions of how it sounds. Half of every playlist comes from the crate. The other half are wildcards the DJ finds on Spotify.

The tools

What it's allowed to touch

It can read files, search Spotify, find similar tracks, get audio features, and validate a proposed playlist.

It can't write files, run commands, browse the web or publish anything. Code creates the playlist, after checking the DJ's work.
